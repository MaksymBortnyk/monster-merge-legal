# Monster Merge --- Privacy Choices & Data Deletion

**Last updated: August 23, 2026**

This page explains the privacy controls available in Monster Merge and
how to request deletion of data.

## Privacy screen

Open **Settings → Privacy** in Monster Merge.

Depending on age classification, region, and Google privacy
requirements, the screen may provide:

-   information about the current Restricted or Adult privacy mode;
-   **Privacy Choices** provided through Google's UMP/CMP;
-   links to the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Support/Privacy
    Requests;
-   the ability to change the locally selected age category.

## Age selection

Monster Merge asks for a neutral age range to apply age-appropriate
privacy and advertising settings.

The selected range is stored locally and is not transmitted to Firebase
Analytics or AdMob as the selected range.

Changing the age category causes the advertising/privacy configuration
to be recalculated. Switching into a Restricted category disables adult
personalized-advertising treatment.

## Advertising choices

Restricted users receive non-personalized/limited age-restricted
advertising and cannot enable personalized advertising.

Adult users may receive Google privacy choices where applicable. Use
**Settings → Privacy → Privacy Choices** when the Google privacy-options
entry point is available to review/change those choices.

## Product analytics

Monster Merge uses privacy-minimized product analytics to understand
gameplay, retention, progression, stability, feature use, and
monetization.

Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection and
advertising-personalization signals are disabled.

Restricted users use limited analytics storage. Where applicable, Adult
analytics storage follows the consent state collected through the
configured Google consent mechanism. When analytics storage is denied,
measurement may continue without device/user analytics identifiers for
aggregated or modeled measurement.

## Premium

Premium removes advertising. Under the production design, AdMob is not
initialized/requested for Premium users.

Product analytics and technical services are separate from the Premium
advertising entitlement and may continue according to the Privacy
Policy.

## Delete local Game data

Monster Merge does not have a user account.

Local game data can be removed using available in-game reset controls or
Android's application-data controls. Removing local data may delete
progress and settings.

A non-consumable Premium purchase is owned through Google Play and may
be restored where supported; clearing local data does not itself cancel
or refund a Google Play purchase.

## Request deletion of provider data

Some data processed through Firebase, Google, or Unity is pseudonymous
and installation-based.

To request deletion or exercise another applicable privacy right, email:

**support.moran@gmail.com**

Subject:

`Monster Merge — Data Deletion Request`

Describe the request and the device/install information available to
you. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested
because legally necessary.

We will determine what information is reasonably required to locate
relevant data and will coordinate provider-specific deletion where
required and technically possible.

## Uninstalling

Uninstalling Monster Merge removes application data stored only on the
device, subject to Android/device backup behavior. Uninstalling does not
necessarily immediately delete data already processed by Google,
Firebase, Unity, Google Play, or other providers; those systems apply
their own retention/deletion processes.

For full details, see the Monster Merge Privacy Policy.

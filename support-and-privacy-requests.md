## Technical support

For a technical problem, it is helpful to provide:

-   device model;
-   Android version;
-   Game version;
-   a description of what happened;
-   steps that reproduce the issue, if known.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or
other sensitive information unless we specifically request information
that is necessary to resolve a legally valid request.

## Purchase support

Premium purchases are processed through Google Play.

For purchase problems, include the type of problem and the approximate
purchase date. Do not email full payment-card information.

Refunds and payment processing are subject to applicable law and Google
Play rules.

## Privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction,
deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or other
available privacy rights.

Send requests to **support.moran@gmail.com** with the subject:

`Monster Merge — Privacy Request`

Monster Merge does not use a user account. Third-party SDK information
is generally pseudonymous and installation-based. We may therefore need
limited technical information sufficient to locate the relevant
installation/provider data. We will not ask for more information than
reasonably necessary to process the request.

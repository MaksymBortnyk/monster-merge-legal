## 1. Scope

Monster Merge does not require a user account and does not ask users
through gameplay to provide their name, email address, telephone number,
postal address, exact date of birth, contacts, photos, messages, or
precise location.

The Game uses technical services that may process pseudonymous
identifiers, device information, usage information, advertising
information, purchase information, and diagnostic information as
described below.

## 2. Age and privacy classification

Monster Merge is designed for a broad audience, including children. On
first launch, the Game asks the user to select a neutral age range:
**0--12, 13--17, or 18+**.

The selected age range is stored locally on the device to apply
age-appropriate privacy and advertising settings. The exact selected age
range is **not sent to Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, or other
third-party SDKs for analytics or advertising**.

For privacy processing, the Game derives a coarse cohort:

-   **Restricted** --- users under 18;
-   **Adult** --- users aged 18 or over.

Where technically applicable, Firebase Analytics may receive only
`restricted` or `adult` for internal product measurement. Users aged
0--12 and 13--17 receive age-restricted advertising treatment
appropriate to their age category. Adult users may receive the standard
adult privacy and advertising flow, subject to applicable law and their
privacy choices.

## 3. Information processed

### Product and gameplay analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand and improve the Game. Analytics
may include app/session lifecycle, runs, score, floor, tier,
progression, merges/drops, character unlocks, feature/booster usage,
game-over/rescue events, ad placement/result events, purchase/Premium
events, duration, and balance/configuration information.

We do not intentionally send names, emails, telephone numbers, exact
ages, selected age ranges, precise location, free-form user text, or our
own cross-device user ID to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection and analytics
advertising-personalization signals are disabled by Game configuration.
Product analytics is not used by us for advertising profiling or
remarketing.

Where applicable law or Google requirements require consent for
installation-level analytics storage, Firebase Consent Mode is used.
When `analytics_storage` is denied, Google may receive measurement
events without device/user analytics identifiers for aggregated or
modeled measurement. When valid consent permits analytics storage,
Firebase may use pseudonymous installation-level identifiers to measure
sessions, retention, funnels, and other product metrics.

Restricted users use limited analytics-storage treatment under the
current production policy.

### Advertising

Monster Merge uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for banner, interstitial,
and rewarded advertisements. The SDK may process IP address/general
location, app and ad interactions, device/app information, diagnostics,
fraud-prevention signals, and advertising/device identifiers where
permitted by age, consent, platform rules, and applicable law.

For users under 18, the Game applies restricted advertising settings.
Personalized or behavioral advertising is not enabled for Restricted
users. The Game configures child/teen treatment as applicable, uses a
child-appropriate maximum ad content rating, and requests
non-personalized or limited advertising. Advertising identifiers must
not be used by the Game's advertising flow for Restricted or unknown-age
users.

For Adult users, Google User Messaging Platform ("UMP") is used where
applicable. Personalized advertising may be used only when permitted by
applicable law, Google policies, and valid privacy choices; otherwise
non-personalized or limited advertising may be shown.

### Crash diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash traces, non-fatal errors, native
crash information, Firebase/Crashlytics installation identifiers, app
version, OS/device characteristics, timestamps, and limited gameplay
diagnostic state such as scene, score, floor, highest tier, and Premium
state.

We use this information to diagnose failures and improve stability. We
do not intentionally include exact age, selected age range, direct
identity information, or free-form user content in Crashlytics reports.

### Remote configuration

Firebase Remote Config provides operational configuration, feature
flags, advertising-frequency settings, balance parameters, and gameplay
configuration. Remote Config uses a Firebase Installation ID. We do not
send the selected age range to Remote Config or use it for behavioral
advertising profiling.

### Purchases

Monster Merge offers a non-consumable Premium purchase through Google
Play using Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play Billing. Purchase
processing may involve product ID, purchase status, entitlement status,
transaction information, Unity installation/player identifiers,
device/session information, and country. Google Play processes
payment-card details; we do not receive or store full payment-card
details.

### Local data

The Game may locally store progress, scores, unlocked content, settings,
onboarding state, Premium entitlement cache, rewarded-ad state, and the
selected age/privacy state. Depending on Android/device backup settings,
local data may be included in device backup/restoration.

Config; comply with law/platform rules; and establish or defend legal
claims.

Where applicable law requires consent for particular storage, analytics,
or advertising processing, we rely on valid consent and provide
available mechanisms to withdraw it. For processing that does not
require consent, we may rely as applicable on providing the requested
service, legal obligations, or legitimate interests in operating,
securing, measuring, debugging, and improving the Game, subject to
applicable rights.

## 5. Children and teenagers

Monster Merge may be used by children and teenagers. We apply more
protective settings to users under 18:

-   the selected age range remains local;
-   Restricted users do not receive personalized/behavioral advertising
    from our configured flow;
-   child/teen advertising treatment is applied as applicable;
-   advertising identifiers are not intended to be transmitted by our
    advertising flow for Restricted users;
-   Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection is disabled globally;
-   product analytics is separated from advertising personalization;
-   Restricted users use limited analytics-storage treatment.

We do not knowingly ask children to submit names, emails, phone numbers,
exact dates of birth, precise location, photos, contacts, chat messages,
or other user-generated personal content.

Parents/guardians may contact support.morun@gmail.com with concerns.

## 7. Advertising and privacy choices

Adult users may receive privacy choices through Google's UMP/CMP
mechanisms where required or available. The Game's Privacy screen may
provide access to **Privacy Choices** where Google's entry point is

Restricted users receive fixed restricted advertising treatment and

Premium prevents the Game from initializing/requesting ads through

## 10. Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the
purposes above, subject to legal, accounting, fraud-prevention,
security, dispute, and platform requirements.

Local data generally remains until cleared/reset or the app is removed,
subject to device backup.

Provider retention varies. Firebase states that Crashlytics keeps crash
stack traces and associated identifiers for approximately 90 days before
beginning deletion; Firebase Installation IDs remain until deletion is
requested through the applicable mechanism, with additional time
potentially required for backups. Unity IAP states that service logs are
retained for 90 days; other purchase/service records may have different
retention periods.

Purchase/accounting records may be retained longer where required by
tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, store, or legal obligations.
